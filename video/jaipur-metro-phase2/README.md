## Revision 2
Photos are now small and gentle: 330×215 px, thin white border, fade in, no pop sound. They sit in the corners away from the route and below the chips, so the line being drawn and the station names stay in view.

## Accuracy
- **Pink Line:** geometry and stations come from OpenStreetMap. Ram Nagar is placed at the midpoint between its neighbours because its coordinates were missing.
- **Orange Line:** this is **indicative**. It is drawn through key stations taken from the supplied route maps (themetrorailguy and the official route map) and placed on OSM Tonk Road and Sikar Road. The screen says "indicative · key stations shown". The exact station list and the Todi Mod section should be confirmed with RMRCL.
- **Facts:**
  - ₹13,037 crore and 41 km: Union Cabinet, 8 April 2026 (DD News, supplied).
  - "~36 stations" and "~2031": sources vary.
  - First stretch Prahladpura→Pinjrapole Gaushala, ~12 km, 10 stations: per published reports.
