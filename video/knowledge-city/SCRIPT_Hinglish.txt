Diggi Malpura Road pe aa raha hai — Knowledge City.

Jaipur ka agla bada sapna. Socho… 10 saal baad — university, medical college, IT institute… aur aaj? Aaj mauka hai — invest karne ka sahi samay.

Yeh hai Knowledge City — Diggi Malpura Road pe, Chittora ke paas.

JDA ki official scheme — Knowledge City South.

January 2026 mein JDA ne iska kabza RSRDC ko saunp diya — project ko mili gati. Lagbhag 1,000 hectare.

Plan suno — university, IT institute, technical institutes, medical college aur hospital, international library, convention centre. Aur desh-videsh ke bade sansthano ko yahan aamantrit kiya jayega.

Yeh idea naya nahi hai. India ke kuch shehron ne yahi kiya — aur dekho kya hua.

Greater Noida — Knowledge Park. Khet the. Aaj wahan dozens of colleges aur universities, lakhon students, aur poora area hostels, PG aur flats se bhara hai.

Sonipat — Education City. Ashoka, OP Jindal jaisi universities aayi — aur ek chhota sa sheher national map pe aa gaya.

Mohali — Knowledge City. IISER jaise research institutes — aur aas-paas naye sectors ban gaye.

Aur humare Rajasthan mein Kota — coaching ne poore sheher ki economy badal di.

Ab socho Jaipur mein… sab ek belt mein. Hazaron students, doctors, teachers — unhe chahiye ghar, hostel, PG, cafe, shops.

Jahan knowledge aata hai — wahan sheher apne aap ban jaata hai.

Par yaad rakhna — abhi early stage hai. Plot lene se pehle official map, approval aur patta zaroor check karo.

Kya Jaipur ka Knowledge City agla Greater Noida banega? Comment karo. Save karo — aur bhejo usko jo future dekh ke invest karta hai.
