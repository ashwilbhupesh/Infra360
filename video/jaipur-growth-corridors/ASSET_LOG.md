# Asset log: Jaipur growth corridors (Ashwillpower)

## Map data
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap, via the Nominatim geocoder (https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org). Positions were looked up on 2026-10-29, roughly one request per second, under the Nominatim usage policy. The data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, credited on the closing card.
- **Raw results:** `osm_places.json`, with the lat/long and OSM display name for every point used.
- **Approximate positions:** Nominatim was rate-limited or returned the wrong place for C-Scheme, Bhankrota and Vaishali Nagar Extension. These use approximate positions, and Malviya Nagar uses a centred position near Gaurav Tower and World Trade Park. All are marked approximate in the builder.
- **How the maps are drawn:** they are original schematic graphics, not traced from any third-party map or satellite image. Each road is a smooth curve through geocoded towns and landmarks along that highway, so roads are indicative, not surveyed alignments.
  - Walled City outline: an approximation through its gates.
  - Southern Ring Road: dashed and indicative.
  - On screen: "Schematic · positions: OpenStreetMap", "Illustrative rings" and "Illustrative · not a forecast".
- **Distances:** straight lines from the Walled City centre (26.924 N, 75.827 E): Vaishali Nagar ≈9 km, Malviya Nagar ≈8 km, Chomu ≈30 km, Bagru ≈31 km, Chaksu ≈37 km, Niwai ≈63 km.
- **Reference images:** the screenshots you supplied (satellite maps, 3D city, the gate photo watermarked "ARYAN O'ZAT") were used only as style references. They are not in the video because their rights are unclear: Google imagery and a watermarked photo.
