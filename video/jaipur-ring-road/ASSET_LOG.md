## Map data
- **Source:** OpenStreetMap via Nominatim, looked up 2026-10-29/30 at about one request per second. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0, credited on the closing card. Raw results are in `osm_places.json`.
- **Approximate positions:** Chitroli (near Bagru, Ajmer Road) and Bagrana (Agra Road) aren't in Nominatim, so they're placed from news descriptions. Bhankrota is also approximate.
- **What is schematic:** both ring roads are original graphics drawn as smooth curves through geocoded points, and are labelled schematic on screen.
  - South Ring Road: through Bhankrota, Shivdaspura and Bagrana.
  - Northern Ring Road: through the surveyed belts (Chitroli → Kalwar → Jalsu side → Rampura Dabri → east → Bagrana).
  - On-screen labels: "Schematic · positions: OpenStreetMap", "Proposed · alignment indicative", "Exits on the proposed road are indicative", "Illustrative · not a forecast".
  - Drawn lengths are about 43 km and 87 km, against the reported 47 km and 99 km. The labels use the reported figures.
