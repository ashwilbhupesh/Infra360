Jaipur is now bigger
than Goa and your plot
zone may have just
changed.
In October 2025,
JDA quietly redrew
Jaipur's map.
Before there were just
14 zones, now 27.
Twenty-five zones plus
Prithviraj Nagar north
and south.
Add them up and the
JDA region is
approximately 6000
square kilometers.
That is four times
Delhi, bigger than all
of Goa.
The Jaipur you know,
that's zones 1 to 9
plus Prithviraj Nagar.
Zone 3 is just 2
sq km,
and the biggest zone
13, Amer and
Jamwaramgarh
at almost six hundred
square kilometers.
Now look at the new
frontier Shahpura,
Chomu, Jalsoo,
Jobner, Dudu,
Phagi,
Madhorajpura,
Chaksu, Bassi,
Toonga, Andhi.
All of this is
JDA now.
And it may not stop
here.
With the master plan
2047,
Bhaskar reports 272
more villages could
join.
So why should you
care?
1.
Approval.
Buy only in
JDA-approved scheme
with proper roads,
a proper layout,
and patta.
2.
Conversion.
Farmlands need 90-A
conversion before you
can build.
3.
Rules.
Your zones and the
master plan decide
what you can build.
Setbacks, FAR,
height, everything.
So quick test.

Vaishali Nagar,
Mansarovar,
Tonk Road.
Which zone are they
in?
