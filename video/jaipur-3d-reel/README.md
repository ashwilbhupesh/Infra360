# Jaipur 3D land prices: countdown reel
`Jaipur_3D_Land_Prices.mp4`: 1080×1920, 54 s, text-led with captions and music. `cover_frame.jpg` is the Instagram cover.

## Flow
| Time (s) | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0 | Cover: "UP TO 1,000× IN 35 YEARS · in 3D" with the city split 1989 \| 2024. |
| 0.6–8 | SAVE/SHARE prompts, "UP TO 1,000×", whole-city slider 1989 → split → 2024, price per gaj then vs now. |

| 12.6–43.6 | Countdown #10 → #1 by multiplier, 3.1 s per area, with the camera flying between them. Order: Jhotwara, Malviya Nagar, Mansarovar, Vaishali, Vidyadhar Nagar, Ajmer Road, Pratap Nagar, Sikar Road, Sitapura, Jagatpura. |

| 49.5–54 | Credits and disclaimer. |
