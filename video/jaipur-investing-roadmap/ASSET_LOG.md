The footage below was checked and downloaded in this project's earlier edits. The Mixkit licence terms were read at https://mixkit.co/license/ (Stock Video Free License). They allow commercial and non-commercial use, including social media video posts, and attribution is not required. ## Original assets made for this video (owned by Ashwillpower)
- **Corridor map (schematic).** Town positions were plotted from approximate latitude and longitude (Jaipur, Kishangarh, Bagru, Bandikui, Kotputli). The route lines are hand-drawn approximations, **not** surveyed or official alignments. On screen it is labelled "Schematic · not to scale · alignments indicative". The Kishangarh–Kotputli expressway is drawn dashed and marked "upcoming", with the line "Verify final alignment & interchanges". "Chitroli" is not placed on the map because its location and spelling are unverified.
- **Original graphics:**

  - the agricultural land checklist;
